Asset type: full-bleed background painting for the hero of a product designer's portfolio website, landscape 16:9, high resolution.

Expressive visible brush marks and thick impasto over woven linen, lyrical French post-impressionist landscape, original composition. Not photorealistic, not 3D, no vector-flat illustration.
Scene: wide sunlit meadow at the edge of a river, distant rolling blue-green hills, a winding pale turquoise river curving from the middle far distance to lower right, tall deep viridian cypress and willow trees along the far right edge, scattered creamy wildflowers and vivid warm orange poppies among green grasses in the lower right foreground. The central-left region from x=5% to 55%, y=18% to 70% must remain very light, low detail and calm, for text readability. Art continues edge to edge with no border, no frame. Asymmetrical, sophisticated composition, not a busy postcard.
Palette: powder blue, pale celadon, butter yellow light, viridian, muted ultramarine, little orange vermilion flowers. Beautiful physical pigment mixing. No violet, purple or magenta.
Text: none. Absolutely no text, letters, signatures, watermark, logo, user interface, frame or typography.
This is artwork ONLY, not a website mockup.

The landscape was supplied as a style reference.

```text

Real alpha transparency across the entire background. The botanical group rises from the lower right corner and angles gently toward the upper left, stems visibly separated, flowers at varied heights, airy stems with lots of empty transparent space between them. Cluster confined to right two thirds of a square 1024x1024 canvas, none of the flowers clipped at top. The bottom of stems may leave frame. Rich physical pigment texture, painterly imperfect marks, deep viridian foliage. No landscape, no horizon, no sky, no ground plane, no opaque background, no frame, no vase, no text, no shadow, no checkered pattern. This PNG will be layered in front of the landscape and gently animated in the wind.
```
